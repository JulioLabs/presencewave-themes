# Still Water

A calm, airy multi-page theme for day spas and massage studios, with home, treatments, spa days and booking pages. The spa day builder adds up treatments and add-ons into a total time and price, for one guest or two side by side, applies a package discount, and carries the chosen day into the booking request form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `treatments` and `addons` lists in `js/main.js` for your menu. The booking form is a demo: connect it to your booking tool or a form service.
