# Golden Hour

A warm, modern single-page theme for full-service hair salons. Its service menu has a stylist level switch (New Talent, Senior, Master) that updates every price and appointment length at once, so guests see what they will pay before they book. It also has a team section, a booking call to action, hours, and a weddings and events block.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `services` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your services, with a price and a time for each stylist level.
