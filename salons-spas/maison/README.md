# Maison

A dark, editorial multi-page theme for luxury salons and color specialists, with atelier, services, stylists and consultation pages. Its new-client color consultation asks four questions (hair today, the goal, length and thickness) and shows a likely price range and the number of visits before the guest books a free consultation.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the prices and factors at the top of `js/main.js` for your own estimates. The request form is a demo: connect it to your booking tool or a form service.
