# Salon, Barber and Spa Themes

Free, responsive website themes for salons, barbers and spas: full-service hair salons, barbershops, day spas and massage studios, nail, lash and brow studios, and luxury color specialists. Every theme contains separate HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and any local assets it needs, and comes with something that turns a visitor into a booking: prices by stylist level, a walk-in board, a spa day builder, a three-step booking request and a color consultation estimate.

## Themes

- **Golden Hour** — warm landing page for hair salons with a service menu that switches prices and times by stylist level.
- **Sharp & Steady** — bold, vintage landing page for barbershops with a walk-in board, the current wait and a join-the-list form.
- **Still Water** — calm multi-page theme for day spas with a spa day builder that adds up time and price.
- **Bloom Bar** — playful landing page for nail, lash and brow studios with a three-step booking request.
- **Maison** — dark, editorial multi-page theme for luxury salons with a color consultation that shows a price range.
