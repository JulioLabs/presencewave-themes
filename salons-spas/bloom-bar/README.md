# Bloom Bar

A bright, playful single-page theme for nail, lash and brow studios. Its three-step booking request lets guests pick a service, an artist who does it (or the first available) and a day and time window, then send their name and number. It also has a colorful service menu, artist profiles and a parties section.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `services`, `artists`, `openDays` and `times` lists in `js/main.js`. The request form is a demo: connect it to your booking tool or a form service.
