# Sharp & Steady

A bold, vintage single-page theme for barbershops. Its walk-in board shows who is cutting today, how long each chair has left, the estimated wait and today's hours, and a join-the-list form adds the guest to the queue and tells them roughly when they're up. It also has a price list, barber profiles and a find-us section.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system. In `js/main.js`, set your opening `hours`, and update `chairs` and `queue` from the counter (or connect them to your booking system) to keep the board current. The join form is a demo: connect it to your queue or a form service.
