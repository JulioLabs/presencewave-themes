# Dental and Medical Clinic Themes

Free, responsive website themes for dental and small medical clinics: family and cosmetic dentists, pediatric dentists, family medicine and walk-in clinics, and physical therapy clinics. Every theme contains separate HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and any local assets it needs, and comes with something that turns a visitor into a patient: an insurance check, a smile cost estimator, a first-visit guide for parents, an open-now status with the walk-in wait, and a "where does it hurt?" picker.

The forms ask for contact details and preferred times only. Keep symptoms and medical history out of website forms; talk about them at the visit.

## Themes

- **Fresh Mint** — bright landing page for family dentists with an insurance check and a new-patient request.
- **Clear Smile** — polished multi-page theme for cosmetic dentists with a smile cost estimator and monthly payments.
- **Little Molars** — playful landing page for pediatric dentists with a first-visit guide by age.
- **Steady Care** — calm multi-page theme for family medicine and walk-in clinics with an open-now status, the walk-in wait and same-day requests.
- **Motion Lab** — energetic landing page for physical therapy clinics with a "where does it hurt?" picker.
