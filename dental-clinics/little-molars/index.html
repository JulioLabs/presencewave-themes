What we do

Gentle care, from first tooth to braces.

Checkups and cleanings

Counting teeth, a tickly polish and a sticker for every brave patient.

Sealants and fluoride

Quick, painless shields that keep cavities away from the back teeth.

Gentle fillings

Laughing gas if they want it, cartoons on the ceiling and lots of breaks.

Orthodontic checks

A spacing check at age 7, so any braces happen at the right time.

For parents

You stay with them, every step.

Parents are welcome in the treatment room for every visit. We explain everything in kid words first, then in grown-up words for you.

  • No scary wordsWe say "sugar bugs" and "tooth pictures", never "drill" or "needle".
  • Siblings togetherBook brothers and sisters back to back in one trip.
  • Most plans welcomeWe file the claim and tell you the cost before any treatment.
