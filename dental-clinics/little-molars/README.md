# Little Molars

A playful single-page theme for pediatric dentists. Its first-visit guide lets parents pick their child's age (under 3, 3 to 5, 6 to 9, 10 to 13) and see what happens at that visit, how long it takes, what to bring and a tip for the day. It also has care cards, a section for parents, a booking call to action, hours and reviews.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `ages` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your own visit steps.
