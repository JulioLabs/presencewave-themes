# Fresh Mint

A bright, friendly single-page theme for family dental practices. Its insurance check lets visitors type their dental plan and see straight away whether the practice is in network, will file the claim for them, or can't take the plan, with the no-insurance price as a fallback. Next to it is a new-patient request (visit type, who's coming, best times) that asks for contact details only. It also has care cards, a dentist team, hours and reviews.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `plans` list at the top of `js/main.js` for the plans you work with. Connect the request form to your scheduling tool, and keep medical details out of it.
