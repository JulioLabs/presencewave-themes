Treatments

Subtle or striking. Always natural.

Every treatment starts with a free consult and a written plan. Prices are typical ranges; yours is confirmed before you begin.

Whitening

from $250

In-office whitening in one 90-minute visit, or custom take-home trays you use for two weeks. Both are gentle on sensitive teeth.

  • Up to eight shades brighter
  • Touch-up gel included

Clear aligners

from $2,800

Nearly invisible aligners for crowding, gaps and crooked teeth. Most plans take six to eighteen months, with a check every eight weeks.

  • 3D preview of your result
  • Retainers included

Porcelain veneers

from $1,100 a tooth

Thin porcelain shells, hand-finished for shape and translucency, for chips, stains and uneven edges.

  • Try your new smile in temporaries first
  • Two visits, about three weeks apart

Cosmetic bonding

from $300 a tooth

Tooth-colored resin sculpted in a single visit to close small gaps or repair chips. No drilling for most cases.

  • Done in about an hour
  • A great first step before veneers
