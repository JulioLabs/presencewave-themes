# Clear Smile

A polished multi-page theme for cosmetic dental studios: home, treatments, smile stories and a consult page. Its smile estimator adds up whitening, clear aligners, veneers and bonding (per tooth, with a tooth count) into a price range and a monthly payment, then leads into a free consult request. Smile stories show before and after shades on the dental shade guide, so the theme needs no photos.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `treatments` list and `MONTHS` at the top of `js/main.js` for your prices and financing term.
