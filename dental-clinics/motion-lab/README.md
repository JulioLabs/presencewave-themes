# Motion Lab

An energetic single-page theme for physical therapy and sports rehab clinics. Its "Where does it hurt?" picker highlights an area on a body map and shows the program most patients start with (typical visits, weeks to goal, what you work on), then fills in the evaluation request below. It also has a four-step "how it works", therapists and hours.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `areas` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your programs (`x` and `y` place each dot on the body map).
