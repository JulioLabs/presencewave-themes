How it works

From first visit to full speed.

  1. 01

    Evaluation

    We test how you move, find what's driving the problem and agree on your goal.

  2. 02

    Hands-on care

    Manual therapy and guided exercise in every visit, never a room full of machines.

  3. 03

    Your home plan

    Short daily exercises in our app, with videos, so progress doesn't stop between visits.

  4. 04

    Back to it

    Return-to-sport testing, so you know you're ready before you run, lift or play again.
