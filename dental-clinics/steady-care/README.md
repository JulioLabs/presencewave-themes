# Steady Care

A calm multi-page theme for family medicine and walk-in clinics: home, services, providers and visit. A status bar on every page shows whether the clinic is open now and when it closes or opens next, worked out from the week's hours. The home page shows an estimated walk-in wait, and the visit page has today's hours highlighted and a same-day visit request that asks for contact details only.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and `hours` and `waitByHour` at the top of `js/main.js` (connect the wait to your check-in system). Keep symptoms and medical details out of the request form.
