# Gridline Clean

A sharp, commercial-first, multi-page theme for cleaning businesses with an operational or facility-management focus.

Pages: Home, Services, Our Standard, and Contact. Open `index.html` to preview, then adjust the variables at the top of `css/styles.css`.
