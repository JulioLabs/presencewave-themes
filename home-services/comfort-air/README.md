# Comfort Air

A clean, bright single-page theme for heating and cooling (HVAC) companies. A season switch changes the hero, the call to action and the accent color between cooling and heating, and it starts in the season that matches the month. Its care plan builder prices a maintenance plan from the number of systems and the level of care, and a repair-or-replace slider gives advice by system age.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` (`--cool` and `--warm` are the two season colors), and the `seasons` text and `tiers` prices at the top of `js/main.js`.
