    FAQ

    Questions we hear a lot

    How often should I service my system?

    Twice a year: the AC in spring and the furnace in fall. A heat pump does both jobs, so it needs both visits.

    How often should I change my filter?

    Every one to three months, more often with pets. Plan members get filters delivered.

    Do you offer financing?

    Yes, from 0% APR for 18 months on new systems, subject to approval.

    Can I cancel the care plan?

    Any time. If you've had a tune-up, we just charge the regular price for it.
