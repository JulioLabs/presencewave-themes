# Dispatch

A bold, navy and safety-orange single-page theme for multi-trade home service companies (plumbing, heating and cooling, electrical) that take emergency calls. It has a 24/7 emergency bar, a ZIP code check that tells visitors whether you serve them and how fast you can be there, and a request-service form that picks the trade, the problem and how urgent it is.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, the `serviceArea` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your own neighborhoods and ZIP codes, and `issues` for the jobs you take.
