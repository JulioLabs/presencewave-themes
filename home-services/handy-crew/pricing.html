Pricing questions

What if we finish early?

For half and full days, we'll find something else on your list or refund unused full hours.

Do you charge for travel?

No, as long as you're inside our area. Further out, there's a flat $25 trip fee.

Who buys the materials?

Either of us. If we pick them up, you pay what we paid and see the receipt.
