# Handy Crew

A warm, practical multi-page theme for handyman and small-repair businesses, with home, services, pricing and booking pages. Pricing is by the visit (a quick fix, a half day or a full day), and the book-a-visit picker shows the next seven open days and the arrival windows still free on each, with a live summary of the visit and its price.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and `visits`, `windows`, `closedDays` and `booked` at the top of `js/main.js` for your own prices and calendar.
