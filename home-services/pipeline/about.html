About us

Three generations of fixing things properly.

Pipeline started with one van in 1998. Today we're a team of fourteen, and every one of us still answers to the same rule: treat each home like it's your grandmother's.

What you can count on

  • On time, or $50 off. If we're late for your window, the discount is automatic.
  • Fixed prices. You approve the price before we start. No hourly surprises.
  • Clean homes. Shoe covers, floor mats and a tidy-up when we're done.
  • Licensed people. Every plumber is licensed, background checked and trained by us.
