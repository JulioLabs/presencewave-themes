# Pipeline

A friendly, trustworthy multi-page theme for family-run plumbing companies, with a home page, services and prices, about and contact pages. Its upfront price guide lets visitors pick a job (a clogged drain, a new water heater, a leak) and see the typical price range, the time it takes and what's included, then book that job in one click.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `jobs` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your own jobs and prices.
