# Live Wire

A dark, high-voltage single-page theme for residential electricians who sell EV chargers, panel upgrades, lighting and backup power. Its project estimator lets visitors tick the projects they're planning and see a typical price range and how long the work takes, then book a free visit. A home safety check section lists the warning signs that bring in older homes.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `projects` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your own projects and prices.
