    FAQ

    Straight answers

    Do I need a permit?

    Most panel, circuit and EV charger work needs one. We pull it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector.

    Can my panel handle an EV charger?

    Often, yes. We do a free load calculation first. If it can't, a load manager is usually cheaper than a new panel.

    Are there rebates?

    Many utilities give rebates for EV chargers and panel upgrades, and there are federal tax credits. We'll tell you what applies.

    Do you do emergency calls?

    Yes. For a power outage in part of the house, sparks or burning smells, call us any time.
