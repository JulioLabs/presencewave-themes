FAQ

Questions, answered.

What if my lawn is bigger than 5,000 sq ft?

We price larger lawns in steps of 1,000 sq ft. You'll see the exact price before your first visit.

Can I start in the middle of the year?

Yes. We start with whatever your lawn needs this season and pick up the calendar from there.

Do you mow on the Essentials and Complete plans?

No, those plans are treatments only. Choose Complete + Mowing if you want us to mow too.

How do I pay?

By card, monthly or yearly. You'll get a receipt by email after every payment.
