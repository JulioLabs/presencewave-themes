We move your visit to the next dry day and text you the new time.

Are your treatments pet safe?

Yes. We use pet-friendly products and tell you how long to keep pets off the grass.

Can I pause over winter?

Mowing pauses automatically when the grass stops growing. You only pay for visits we make.
