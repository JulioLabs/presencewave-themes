# Personal Best

A clean, personal single-page theme for independent personal trainers, with a goal finder that recommends a program and price from three questions, training packages, client results and a free consult form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `programs` list at the top of `js/main.js` to describe your own programs and prices.
