# Neighborhood Gym

A friendly, practical multi-page theme for community gyms (home, memberships, classes, visit us), with a membership builder that adds family members and extras to a monthly price, a filterable class timetable, community events and a free tour form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `prices` and `classes` lists in `js/main.js` to set your own plans and timetable.
