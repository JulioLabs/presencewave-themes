First visit

No ego. No judgement. Just a plan.

Never touched a barbell? Good. Most of our members hadn't either. Your first three days are on us.

  1. Day 1: Movement screen. A coach checks how you move and talks through your goals.

  2. Day 2: Foundations class. Learn the squat, hinge and press with an empty bar.

  3. Day 3: Join a class. Train with the group and see if it's for you.
