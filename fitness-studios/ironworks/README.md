# Ironworks

A gritty, industrial single-page theme for strength gyms and conditioning boxes, with a membership comparison table, a monthly or annual price toggle, a first-visit plan, a PR board and a free 3-day pass form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `data-monthly` and `data-annual` prices in `index.html` to set your own membership prices.
