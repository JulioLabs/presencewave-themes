      FAQ

      Before your first class

      I'm not fit yet. Is that a problem?

      Not at all. Every move has an easier option and coaches show it first. Most members started exactly where you are.

      What should I bring?

      Water and something comfortable to move in. We have shoes for Rhythm Ride, towels and lockers.

      How do I cancel a booking?

      Cancel up to 8 hours before class from your confirmation email, and your spot goes to the waitlist.

      Is there parking?

      Yes, free parking behind the studio and bike racks out front.
