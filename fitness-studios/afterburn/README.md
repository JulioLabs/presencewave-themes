# Afterburn

A bold, dark, high-energy single-page theme for boutique HIIT, ride and strength studios, with a weekly class schedule by day, spots-left badges, a waitlist for full classes, an intro offer and a book-a-free-class form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `schedule` list at the top of `js/main.js` to show your own classes.
