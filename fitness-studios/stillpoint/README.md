# Stillpoint

A calm, airy multi-page theme for yoga and pilates studios (home, schedule, pricing, contact), with a timetable you can filter by style and level, a planner that shows whether a class pack or a membership is better value, and an intro offer form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `classes` list at the top of `js/main.js` to show your own timetable.
