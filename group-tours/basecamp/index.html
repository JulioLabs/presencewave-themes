Activity levels

Honest grading, so nobody's left behind

1

Easy

3–5 miles a day on good trails, little climbing. If you walk the dog for an hour, you're ready.

2

Moderate

6–9 miles and up to 2,000 ft of climbing a day. Regular hikers and gym-goers enjoy these most.

3

Challenging

10+ miles, steep ground and altitude. For people who hike most weekends and want a real summit.
