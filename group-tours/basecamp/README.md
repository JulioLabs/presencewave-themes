# Basecamp

A rugged single-page theme for small-group hiking and adventure trips, with topographic visuals, a live trip finder (region, month and activity level), honest activity grading and an ask-a-guide form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `trips` list at the top of `js/main.js` to show your own trips.
