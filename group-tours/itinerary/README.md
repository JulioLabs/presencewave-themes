# Itinerary

An editorial, multi-page theme for escorted cultural and food tours: a home page, a journeys listing with places left per date, a sample trip page with a day-by-day itinerary and a reserve-with-deposit form that works out the deposit and balance, an about page and a contact page.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `pricing` values at the top of `js/main.js` for your deposit and room prices.
