Our way

Travel that leaves room to notice things

We started in 2004 with one journey through Tuscany and a rule we still keep: never more than 18 guests, never more than two hotel changes a week.

Our tour directors are historians, sommeliers and former chefs. They're joined on every journey by local specialists: the archaeologist who excavated the site, the winemaker who pours, the grandmother who teaches the pasta.

We pay our partners fairly, eat where locals eat, and choose family-run hotels wherever we can.
