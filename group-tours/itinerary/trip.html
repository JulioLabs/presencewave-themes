Italy · 10 days · 12 to 18 guests

Tuscany & Umbria at the Table

Ten days of farmhouse lunches, cellar doors and hill towns, from Florence to Rome, with a tour director who's lived in Tuscany for twenty years.

From$5,890
Deposit$750
Single room+$990

Day by day

Day 1Florence, arrival

Meet at the hotel at 5pm for a welcome aperitivo on the terrace, then dinner in the Oltrarno.

Meals: Dinner

Day 2Florence, the Renaissance

A morning in the Uffizi with an art historian, free afternoon, and a private visit to a family silk workshop.

Meals: Breakfast

Day 3Chianti

Drive through the Chianti hills to a farmhouse for a cooking class: hand-made pici and tiramisu. Overnight in a villa.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 4Montalcino & Brunello

Cellar tour and tasting at a Brunello estate, lunch among the vines, sunset in Pienza.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 5Truffle hunting

Out with a truffle hunter and his dogs at dawn, then a truffle lunch. Afternoon at leisure by the pool.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 6Orvieto

The cathedral and the underground city, then on to Umbria. Dinner at an agriturismo.

Meals: Breakfast, dinner

Day 7Assisi

The Basilica of St Francis with a local guide, an olive oil mill, and a free evening.

Meals: Breakfast

Day 8Perugia

Chocolate workshop and the hill town on foot. Farewell dinner with music.

Meals: Breakfast, dinner

Day 9Rome, departure

Transfer to Rome Fiumicino or the city centre by noon.

Meals: Breakfast
