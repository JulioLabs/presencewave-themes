# Getaway Club

A bright, social single-page theme for group vacations, with a departure calendar by month, seats-left badges, a waitlist for sold-out dates and a reserve-a-seat form.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `departures` list at the top of `js/main.js` to show your own dates.
