    FAQ

    Good questions, honest answers

    Who travels with Getaway Club?

    Mostly people in their 40s to 70s, about two thirds traveling solo. Groups are small enough that nobody gets lost in the crowd.

    How active are the trips?

    Each trip has an activity level from 1 (easy strolls) to 3 (several hours of walking on uneven ground). It's on every departure.

    Are flights included?

    No, so you can fly from wherever suits you. We meet you at the arrival airport and share recommended flights with the group.

    What if a trip is sold out?

    Join the waitlist from the departure calendar. You're offered seats in the order you joined, and there's no charge until you accept.
