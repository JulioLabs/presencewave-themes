# Grand Tour

A dark, elegant single-page theme for slow rail and luxury group journeys, with a numbered journey collection, a side-by-side journey comparison (length, price per day, hotels, pace, season) and a brochure request by email or post.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `journeys` list at the top of `js/main.js` to compare your own journeys.
