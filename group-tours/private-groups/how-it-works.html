How it works

From first call to welcome home

One planner stays with your group from the first quote to the last airport transfer.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your group

    Size, dates, budget and what the trip is for. Use the quote builder or call the group desk.

  2. 2

    Get a written proposal

    A day-by-day itinerary with a fixed per-person price, within two working days. Change anything until it fits.

  3. 3

    Sign up your travelers

    We give you a private sign-up page for your group. Travelers pay their own deposits; you see who's in.

  4. 4

    Travel with a tour manager

    A tour manager travels with groups of 20 or more, and our support line is open around the clock.

Organizer questions

Is there a minimum group size?

Ten travelers. Below that, ask about our small-group departures.

Do organizers travel free?

Yes: one free place for every 15 paying travelers, plus a planning call whenever you need one.

What if travelers drop out?

Your price is fixed for a group size range, so a few cancellations don't change it.
