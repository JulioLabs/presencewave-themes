# Private Groups

A clean, practical multi-page theme for custom group trips (schools, clubs, faith groups and companies): a home page with a group trip quote builder (trip type, group size, nights and comfort level give a price range per person and free organizer places), a how-it-works page, trip ideas and a contact page.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the rates at the top of `js/main.js` for your own prices.
