# Open Door

A bold, colorful single-page theme for real estate agents who live on open houses. It lists this weekend's open houses (the dates always fall on the coming weekend), each with an RSVP button that picks the house in the RSVP form and an "Add to calendar" button that downloads an `.ics` file with the time, the address and a reminder. It also has a "how open houses work" section, a pitch to sellers with open house numbers, reviews and "Equal Housing Opportunity" in the footer.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `openHouses` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your open houses. Connect the RSVP form to your CRM.
