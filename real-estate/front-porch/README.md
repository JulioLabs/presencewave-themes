# Front Porch

A warm, friendly single-page theme for neighborhood real estate agents. Its home value estimate lets a homeowner pick their neighborhood, enter the size, bedrooms and condition, and see a price range straight away, with a link that carries the neighborhood into a free market analysis request. It also has buying and selling services, featured listings, an agent profile, a market snapshot and reviews, and "Equal Housing Opportunity" in the footer.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `areas` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your neighborhoods and recent prices per square foot. The estimate is labelled as an estimate, not an appraisal. Connect the contact form to your CRM.
