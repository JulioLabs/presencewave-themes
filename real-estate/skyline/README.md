# Skyline

A sleek, dark multi-page theme for luxury and condo brokerages: home, listings, neighborhoods and contact. Its listing search filters the residences by type, price and bedrooms (also from a quick search on the home page) and sorts them by date or price. "Book a showing" on any listing carries that residence to the contact page, where it is shown above the form. It also has featured residences, the brokerage's numbers, its private-sale approach, neighborhood guides and "Equal Housing Opportunity" in the footer.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `listings` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your residences. Connect the contact form to your CRM.
