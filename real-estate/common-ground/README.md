# Common Ground

An editorial multi-page theme for relocation agents and anyone who sells a city as much as a house: home, neighborhoods, moving guides and contact. Its neighborhood compare puts two neighborhoods side by side (median price, the commute downtown, walkability, parks nearby and typical homes), ticks the better figure in each row and carries both names into the contact form. The neighborhoods page adds a card for each one, and the guides page has a monthly guide signup. "Equal Housing Opportunity" is in the footer.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `hoods` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your neighborhoods. Describe homes, streets and amenities, never the people who live there. Connect the contact and signup forms to your CRM or newsletter.
