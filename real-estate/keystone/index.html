      1 week

    2. 2

    3. 3

    Questions

    The ones everyone asks.

    How much do I need saved?

    The down payment (3–20% of the price), closing costs (about 2–4%) and a cushion for moving in. Programs can cover some of it.

    What does working with you cost?

    Our fee and who pays it are agreed in writing before we start. We'll go through it at the consultation, with no surprises at closing.

    Does my credit score need to be perfect?

    No. Many loans start at a score of 620, some lower. A lender can tell you where you stand in a single call.

    Can I buy if I'm self-employed?

    Yes. Lenders look at two years of tax returns instead of pay stubs. Start gathering them early.
