# Keystone

A steady, reassuring single-page theme for agents who specialise in first-time home buyers. Its mortgage payment calculator turns the home price, down payment, rate, term, property tax, insurance and HOA into a monthly payment, with mortgage insurance added below 20% down, a breakdown bar, the loan amount, the cash needed and rough closing costs. "Talk through these numbers" carries the price into a free consultation request. It also has the five steps from renting to owning, down payment programs, an FAQ and "Equal Housing Opportunity" in the footer.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the defaults in the form and `PMI_RATE` in `js/main.js` for your market. The result is labelled as an estimate, not a loan offer. Connect the consultation form to your calendar or CRM.
