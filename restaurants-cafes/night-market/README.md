# Night Market

A dark, neon single-page theme for street food stalls and food trucks. It has a weekly "where to find us" schedule that starts with today and badges the current stop, and a build-your-bowl calculator (base, protein, toppings and sauce) with a live receipt and price.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, the `stops` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your weekly locations, and `options` for your bowl ingredients and prices.
