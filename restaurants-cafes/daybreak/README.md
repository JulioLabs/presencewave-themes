# Daybreak

A sunny, warm single-page theme for all-day cafés and brunch spots. It shows an "open now" badge worked out from your opening hours, highlights today in the hours table, and has a menu with Breakfast, Lunch and Drinks tabs that diners can filter by vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, the `hours` list at the top of `js/main.js` for your opening hours, and `menu` for your dishes, prices and dietary tags.
