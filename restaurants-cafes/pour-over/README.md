# Pour Over

A calm, minimal single-page theme for coffee roasters and espresso bars. It lists this season's coffees with tasting notes and roast level, and has a coffee subscription builder: coffee, bag size, grind and how often, with the price per delivery and per month.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `subscription` settings at the top of `js/main.js` for your coffees, bag sizes, discounts and prices.
