Brew guides

Three ways we like it.

Pour over

15g · 250ml · 3 min

Medium-fine grind, 94°C water. Bloom 30 seconds, then pour in slow circles.

French press

30g · 500ml · 4 min

Coarse grind. Stir once at a minute, skim the crust at four, press gently.

Espresso

18g in · 36g out · 28 s

Fine grind. If it runs fast and sour, go finer; slow and bitter, go coarser.
