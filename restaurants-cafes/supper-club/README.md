# Supper Club

A dark, elegant multi-page theme for tasting-menu and fine-dining restaurants: home, menus, private events and reserve. The private events page has an estimator: guests choose the number of guests, a menu, drinks and extras, and see the total with service and the deposit that holds the date.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `eventOptions` settings at the top of `js/main.js` for your menus, pairings, extras, service charge and deposit.
