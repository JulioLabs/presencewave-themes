La nostra storia

Three generations, one kitchen.

Nonna Rosa opened the doors in 1987 with eight tables and one rule: nobody leaves hungry.

It started with a pasta board.

Rosa brought her board, her rolling pin and her mother's recipes from Bologna. Her son Paolo built the wood oven. Today her granddaughter Giulia runs the kitchen, and the board is still there, by the window, every afternoon at 3.

We buy tomatoes, greens and herbs from three farms within 40 miles, make our own pasta, bread and desserts, and still roll the gnocchi by hand.
