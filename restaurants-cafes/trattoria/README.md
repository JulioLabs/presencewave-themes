# Trattoria

A classic, warm multi-page theme for family restaurants: home, menu, our story and reserve. The reserve page has a table request picker: guests choose a party size, one of the next seven days (closed days are crossed out) and an open time slot, and see a summary of their table before they send the request.

Open `index.html` to preview. Customize the variables at the top of `css/styles.css` to change its visual system, and the `reservations` settings at the top of `js/main.js` for your closed days, seating times and largest party. The menu is plain HTML in `menu.html`.
